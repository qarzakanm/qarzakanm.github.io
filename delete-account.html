Delete Qarzakanm Account & Data

If you have connected a Google account to the Qarzakanm (قەرزەکانم) application, you can request deletion of your account and associated data by following the instructions below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to qarzakanm@gmail.com using the same email address associated with your Qarzakanm Google account.
  2. Use the following subject: Delete My Qarzakanm Account
  3. In the email, clearly state that you want to delete your Qarzakanm account and associated data.
Important: Qarzakanm does not require an account to use the main loan and debt notebook features. A Google account is only connected when you voluntarily use Google Sign-In and is used for authentication and optional backup and restore functionality.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When your account deletion request is processed, we will delete the account-related information that Qarzakanm maintains through Firebase Authentication, including:

  • Google/Firebase account identifier
  • Email address associated with the account
  • Display name, if available
  • Other authentication-related information associated with the Qarzakanm account

Google Drive Backup Data

If you have used the Qarzakanm Google Drive Backup feature, your Qarzakanm backup may be stored in Google's application-data storage associated with your Google account.

The backup may contain information that you entered into Qarzakanm, including:

  • Loan records
  • Debt records
  • Payment records
  • Names
  • Dates
  • Notes
  • Other Qarzakanm records stored in the application database

As part of an account deletion request, we will take reasonable steps to delete Qarzakanm backup data associated with the account where it is under our control.

Local Data on Your Device

Qarzakanm primarily operates offline. Your loan and debt records are normally stored locally on your Android device.

Qarzakanm does not maintain a separate server database containing your local loan records for normal App functionality.

Important: Deleting your Qarzakanm account does not automatically delete data that is stored only on your device. You should delete your local Qarzakanm records using the available deletion options in the App or clear the App's data/uninstall the App if you want to remove locally stored information.

What Will Not Be Deleted?

Qarzakanm does not control your general Google account, Google Drive files outside of the Qarzakanm application-data area, or other information maintained by Google.

Deleting your Qarzakanm account does not delete your entire Google account or your other Google Drive files.

Processing Time

We will review account deletion requests and process eligible requests within a reasonable period.

Once account-related data has been deleted, it may not be possible to restore it.
